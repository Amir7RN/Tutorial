Fat: more joints than demands

Every demand is reachable, in many ways. Fix the push first, then let the joints feel it.

  1. (JJᵀ)⁻¹ in three moves: split V along the no-leak hand axes u₁, u₂ · divide each part by its σ² · recombine. Result: the corrected push w.
  2. Jᵀ: each joint spins by the torque it feels from w.
  3. Exact tip velocity, least joint motion, no fighting.

θ̇ = Jᵀ (JJᵀ)⁻¹ V

Tall: fewer joints than demands

Only some demands are reachable. Let the joints feel V first, then divide.

  1. Jᵀ: each joint reads its share of V. Whatever no joint can feel is dropped here.
  2. (JᵀJ)⁻¹ in three moves: split the shares along the no-overlap joint mixes v₁, v₂ · divide each by its σ² · recombine. Result: joint speeds.
  3. Reachable demand: exact. Unreachable: the closest the arm can do.

θ̇ = (JᵀJ)⁻¹ Jᵀ V

SVD: one recipe for every shape

  1. Uᵀ: split V along the ellipse axes u₁, u₂.
  2. Σ⁺: divide each part by its σ (skip σ = 0).
  3. V: each part becomes its joint mix; add them.

θ̇ = V Σ⁺ Uᵀ V_d (equals both formulas above)

Near a singularity: λ²

  1. Divide by (σ² + λ²) instead of σ².
  2. Healthy direction: λ² barely matters.
  3. Weak direction: the answer fades to 0 instead of blowing up.

θ̇ = (JᵀJ + λ²I)⁻¹ JᵀV
