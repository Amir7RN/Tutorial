how to read the plots

Top: the lean (ankle angle) is the thing to be saved; the torso and arm angles are what was spent to save it. Middle: each link's share of the angular momentum about the ankle. The total only changes through the ankle torque, gravity and the push; the controller can only move it between links and shift the COM so that gravity works for it. Bottom: the torques, each against its motor's limit.
