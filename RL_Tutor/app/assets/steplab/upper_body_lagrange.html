Sagittal plane: from screw axes to τ = M q̈ + c + g

The simplified G1 in 3-D: four rigid bodies on a pinned ankle. Every step is split into small sub-steps (the letters under it). Each sub-step does one computation, shows its numbers on the right and only its vectors on the robot. next ▶ walks through all of them. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

goal and plan

Goal: the four joint torques τ = M(q) q̈ + c(q, q̇) + g(q). Each term is a sum over the four bodies, so we need one thing per body: its body Jacobian Jb,i at its centre of mass (3 rows × 4 columns, one column per joint). Four bodies, four Jacobians. There is no Jacobian "per joint"; the joints are the columns.

  1. Model: four bodies; the COM of each body carries a frame {i}.
  2. One screw axis 𝒮j per joint, read off the robot at home. Used in 3 and 4.
  3. For each body: home pose Mi, then Tsi(q) = e[𝒮0]q0⋯e[𝒮i]qiMi.
  4. For each body: Js,i from the moved axes, then Jb,i = [AdTis] Js,i using Tsi from 3.
  5. M = Σi Jb,iᵀ 𝒢i Jb,i: four terms (page 165).
  6. g = −Σi Jb,iᵀ 𝓕g,i: the weights, four terms.
  7. c = Σi Jb,iᵀ 𝓕vel,i: the velocity products, four terms.
  8. τ = M q̈ + c + g.
